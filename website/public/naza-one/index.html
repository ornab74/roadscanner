Help shape Naza One + Roadscanner

Want to try Naza One or the new Roadscanner app on Android?

Both apps are available through closed beta testing. Join the Google testing group first, then use the Play Store test link for the app you want to install.

STEP 1

Join the Google testers group

Open the group, sign in with the Google account you use on your Android phone, then tap Join group.

Join the Google group

Android beta approval may take up to 10 days. After joining the testers group, use the same Google account when opening the Play Store test links below. Once your account is approved, the Play Store page will let you become a tester and install the app.

STEP 2

Choose the Android app you want to test

Open the test page for Naza One or Roadscanner. When access becomes available, tap the button to become a tester, then install the app directly from Google Play.
